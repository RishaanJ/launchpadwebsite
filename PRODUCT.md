# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

High school students who want to build real products, educators and chapter leaders who support them, and sponsors, founders, and operators who can fund or mentor their work.

## Product Purpose

Launchpad is a product-development network that helps high school students build, ship, sell, and scale products with real users. The site explains the mission, recruits students and chapter leaders, and gives prospective partners a credible path to support the organization.

## Brand Personality

Ambitious, direct, and builder-led. The experience should feel credible enough for institutional partners while retaining the urgency and optimism of a student-founded organization.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic education-nonprofit sentimentality, polished-but-empty startup templates, hackathon trophy culture, and visual language that makes student work feel hypothetical or juvenile.

## Design Principles

- Lead with shipped outcomes rather than abstract promises.
- Treat students as capable builders, not passive learners.
- Make ambition concrete through programs, milestones, and public proof.
- Give every audience a clear next action.
- Keep institutional credibility without sanding away the organization’s student-led energy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA, preserve visible keyboard focus, maintain readable contrast, respect reduced-motion preferences, and keep core navigation and calls to action usable without animation or color alone.
