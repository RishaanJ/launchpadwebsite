# Launchpad Sponsorship — Outreach Emails

Three templates. Plain text, no links or attachments in the first email (MLH best practice: spam filters, skimming execs). Variants for company contacts and individual founders.

---

## Email 1: First Touch

### Variant A — Company Contact (DevRel/Marketing/People team)

```
Subject: 15 min about Launchpad — founding sponsor seats open

Hi [Name],

We're building the pipeline for high school builders in the Bay Area. Teams spend four sprints shipped shipping real products in front of real users — not class projects, not trophies. The Shipathon lands Spring 2027.

We're asking six companies to back it from the beginning. There are six seats. We're talking to each one once.

A 15-minute call closes this. Happy to work around your calendar.

Can you talk this week?

[Your name]
Launchpad
```

### Variant B — Individual Founder/Operator

Hi [Name],

## Sending Notes

- **Email 1:** Send plain-text in-body. No HTML, no images, no links. Let them reply if interested.
- **Email 2:** Triggered on first reply or after 2–3 days if no reply. Attach the one-pager PDF + full deck.
- **Email 3:** Send exactly 7 days after Email 1. Makes the deadline concrete.
- **Personalization:** Use their actual name, title, and company. Add one-line context if you know it (e.g., "noticed your team sponsors hackathons" or "your talk on shipping resonated").
- **Company vs. founder:** Use Variant A for company email addresses or verified corporate roles; Variant B for founders, operators, or individuals.
