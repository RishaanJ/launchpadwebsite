# Launchpad Founding Sponsor Prospectus

## PAGE 1

---

### What is Launchpad?

The high school product development network. We teach high schoolers to build, ship, sell, and scale real products that reach real people.

Not class projects. Not hackathon trophies. Real.

**The gap isn't talent.** Schools can teach coding. They can't teach shipping — the entire loop from "I have an idea" to "someone uses this." No mentors who've done it. No money that rewards doing it. No room where shipping is the default. 

We're closing that gap. Shipping is a skill. Nobody is teaching it. We are.

---

### Who We Reach

The founding chapter is live at AHS in the Bay Area. Open meetings draw 20+ attendees monthly.

- **20+** students at open public meetings  
- **Monthly** founder talks with working founders  
- **Spring 2027** first annual Bay Area Shipathon  

The founding cohort becomes the national team — the people who write the chapter playbook every future school runs on.

---

### Year One

**Chapters** → School-based teams where builders start.

**Cohorts** → Four sprints across the year. Build, Ship, Sell, Scale. Milestones unlock grants.

**Shipathon** → 48 hours from idea to live product, Spring 2027. Judged by working founders.

**Ignition** → The fund. Unrestricted angel checks of $1K–$5K for teams that earned them. First checks Fall 2027.

---

### Where Your Money Goes

**$30–40K goal.** Every dollar routed through a 501(c)(3), every line item visible.

| Tier | Amount | Funds | Per-Tier Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Friend** | $500 | Scholarship unlocks, materials | 5 micro-grants that ship teams toward milestone 2 |
| **Builder** | $2,500 | Cohort mentorship, chapter materials | Ships hardware to new chapters; matches 1 founder mentor for a team |
| **Operator** | $10,000 | Shipathon venue & tracks, Atlas pilot | Funds one regional Shipathon site end-to-end; underlies 10 micro-grants globally |
| **Title** | $30,000+ | Full year operations + growth | Underwrites entire Shipathon, names a track or Ignition cohort, funds Ignition batch |

All tiers receive post-event impact reports: projects shipped, student reach, photos, outcomes.
