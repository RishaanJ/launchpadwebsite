## Remaining Fixable Issues

### P2 — One Issue

**Slide 8, Row 4: Operator ($10K) and Title ($30K+) both read "Top placement" for logo on site**

The row "Logo on site, newsletter + event materials" shows: Friend `—`, Builder `/`, Operator `Top placement`, Title `Top placement`. Both upper tiers carry identical cell text. A potential Title sponsor paying 3× the Operator rate will notice this and it creates a momentary hesitation — "if my logo is placed the same as a $10K sponsor, what am I paying the extra $20K for?" The answer is obvious from the other 5 rows where Title differentiates clearly (Naming partner, Founder-talk slot, Judge seat, Category exclusivity), but the identical logo-row label is still a visual blip a sharp CMO will flag internally.

**Fix:** Change the Title column cell for Row 4 from "Top placement" to "Premier / title listing" or "Marquee listing." One cell, 10 seconds.
