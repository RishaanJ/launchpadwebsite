## Prioritized Fix List — Remaining Issues

### P1 — Fix Before Sending

**1. "CHAPTER 0" stat reads as zero chapters, not a chapter name (Slide 5)**

The stat row uses the large-number format: `CHAPTER 0 / live at AHS, Bay Area — the founding chapter`. To anyone unfamiliar with Launchpad's naming convention, "CHAPTER 0" in a big stat block reads as "we have zero chapters." The label saves it, but only if the reader slows down. The number-format convention creates false ambiguity — the "0" looks like a count, not a name. Fix: rename the stat `1 CHAPTER` with the label "Chapter Zero: live at AHS, Bay Area — the founding chapter" or use a text stat like `CHAPTER ZERO` in a non-numeric treatment.

**2. Benefits matrix: "Listed on launchpad.org + materials" shows identical treatment for all four tiers (Slide 8)**

All four tiers get "/" (yes) in this row with no visible differentiation. But Friend gets name-only, Builder gets logo + event materials, Operator and Title get top placement. That hierarchy is gone. Right now a $500 Friend appears to get the same website presence as a $30,000 Title sponsor. This will be caught by any CFO reviewing the matrix and will kill the Operator/Title pitch. Fix: split into two rows ("Name on website" for all tiers; "Logo on site, newsletter, chapter materials" for Builder+) or add a text qualifier per cell.

**3. Benefits matrix: Builder tier has no recruiting or demo-day access (Slide 8)**

The row "First look at demo days + recruiting access" shows `—` for both Friend and Builder. In the original research and one-pager, Builder received invite access to demo days. Removing it strips the only talent-pipeline benefit from a $2,500 tier that a company sponsor would actually use. For a DevRel budget, "Builder" needs at least one concrete talent-adjacent perk. Fix: give Builder an invite-level entry ("Invite to Ignition demo day") and keep the richer access for Operator/Title.

**4. "OVER-GOAL" stat breaks the numeric stat row convention (Slide 12)**

The three stats are `6 / founding seats`, `$30–40K / closes the entire year`, and `OVER-GOAL / every dollar past goal seeds the 2027 Ignition fund`. The first two are numbers; the third is a label. In the large-stat design, "OVER-GOAL" renders as oversized label text, which is jarring next to two numbers. Fix: reformulate as `$30K+` with the label "any over-goal seeds the 2027 Ignition fund" — or just use `>GOAL` in the mono font.

**5. 7-day seat hold is only on Slide 12, not the ask slide (Slide 7)**

The urgency mechanism ("We'll hold a seat for 7 days while we talk") appears on Slide 12 but not Slide 7 where the tiers are first shown. A sponsor who stops at the tier slide — which many will — misses the hold offer entirely. Fix: add one line to the Slide 7 callout or the tier card footer: "We'll hold your seat for 7 days."

### P2 — Meaningful Improvements

**6. Cover headline is a regression (Slide 1)**

"Sponsorship Deck." is accurate but flat. The original had "Sponsor the next generation." which led with mission and earned a CMO's attention. The new cover opens with a document type, not a proposition. A CMO's inbox is full of decks labeled "Sponsorship Deck." Fix: restore an emotionally resonant headline on the cover. "Sponsor the next generation." or "Back the builders." beats filing-cabinet language.

**7. Over-goal/Ignition relationship needs one clarifying sentence (Slides 10 and 12)**

Slide 10 says "$25K raises the 2027 fund — separate from the year-one goal." Slide 12 says "every dollar past goal seeds the 2027 Ignition fund." These don't quite add up: if Ignition is a separate $25K raise, what does "seeding" with over-goal money mean — does it reduce the separate ask, match it, or add to it? A reader who reads both slides carefully will be confused. Fix: one sentence on either Slide 11 or Slide 12: "Over-goal contributions go directly into the $25K Ignition 2027 fund, reducing what we need to raise separately."

**8. Shipathon slide still has no named judges even as placeholders (Slide 9)**

"Venue and judge roster are being locked now" is better than before, but "being locked" is passive and indefinite. One placeholder judge name with example text ("e.g. [Name], founder of [Company] — first confirmed judge") — even in amber like the team card — would signal that this process is real and active rather than aspirational. The team slide correctly uses amber placeholders; the Shipathon slide could do the same.

**9. Slide 5 callout references HCB ledger but the traction stat still has no shipped-product proof**

The callout update ("our books are public in real time on Hack Club Bank") is good, but the three stats still contain zero evidence that any student has shipped a product. "20+ attendees" at an open meeting is a club, not a program. Even one placeholder line like "First cohort team shipping [product category]: [Month]" or a note like "Founding cohort: 4 enrolled teams, 2 at Build sprint" would materially change the traction story. This is the hardest stat to fix without real data, but worth flagging — a CMO skimming this slide still stops here.
